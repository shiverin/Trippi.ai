That error's pretty self-explanatory once you spot it at the bottom — Oracle's just out of physical hosts for that shape in that AD right now. It's super common with VM.Standard.E2.1.Micro specifically since it's the Always Free shape and everyone's fighting over the same limited capacity pool.

A few ways to fix it:

1. Try a different Availability Domain
You only have AD-1 selected. If your region has multiple ADs (check top right of console), switch to AD-2 or AD-3 in the Placement section and retry.

2. Remove the Fault Domain pin (if set)
You've already got it on "Let Oracle choose" — good, that's not the issue here.

3. Just retry
Capacity frees up as other free-tier instances get terminated. Sometimes spamming "Create" every few minutes for a bit works. Annoying but true.

4. Switch to ARM Ampere instead
If you don't specifically need the AMD E2 micro shape, VM.Standard.A1.Flex (Ampere ARM, also Always Free, up to 4 OCPU/24GB) tends to have much better capacity availability. Way better specs too for a backend server. Unless you have a hard reason to need x86, I'd just switch shapes.

5. Try a different region entirely
If you're flexible, regions with less free-tier demand (less popular ones) tend to have capacity more often.

6. Use the CLI/Terraform with retry logic
Some people script a loop that retries instance creation every 60s until it succeeds, since capacity opens up sporadically.

Given you're running trippi-backend-01 — if it doesn't need to be x86 specifically, I'd go with option 4 first (A1.Flex), it's usually the path of least resistance. Want me to walk through what to change in your current config to switch shapes?
